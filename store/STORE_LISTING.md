Swing, bounce and fling your way through a bright candy sky in Jelly Swing Arcade!

Tap to hop, hold to grab the nearest orb and swing, then let go to launch yourself across the sky. Scoop up coins, dodge sneaky bats and sticky spider webs, and see how far your jelly can go.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Coin Run: a 90-second race against other jellies. Whoever has the most coins when time runs out wins!
• Sky Climb: swing higher and higher past famous places like the Pyramids, the Eiffel Tower and Stonehenge, all the way to outer space.
• Jelly Tag: don't get stuck being IT! Tag another jelly before your 30 seconds run out, and be the last jelly standing to win.

COLLECT THEM ALL
• 50 cute jellies across five rarities, plus limited-time seasonal jellies for Fall, Winter, Spring and Summer.
• Open chests with the coins you earn, then claim your new jelly.
• The Sparkle chest unlocks glows and trails: hearts, bubbles, rainbows, confetti and more.

FUN EXTRAS
• Power-ups: rocket boost, warp, coin magnet and spinning knives. Grab them in the sky or buy them with coins.
• The shop-only Wild Rocket flings you in a random direction!
• A lucky spin after every run: always win coins, and sometimes a revive.
• Pick your own name, follow the quick How to Play guide, and play with simple one-touch controls.

Jelly Swing Arcade is free to play. It shows ads from Google AdMob (family-friendly and non-personalized); Google may collect some device data to show ads. See the privacy policy for details. No account needed, and no in-app purchases.

## App Review information

- Sign-in required: **No**
- Notes for the reviewer:
  > No login is needed. Jellies, effects and power-ups are unlocked with coins earned in the game; there are no in-app purchases.
  > No login is needed. Tap to jump, hold to swing from the purple orbs. There is no server, no remote configuration and no feature flags: every feature is in the binary and available offline from the first launch.
  > Ads are served by Google AdMob with child-directed treatment and G-rated content only. This is a new AdMob account, so ads may not fill until the app is live; the game then shows "Ad not ready" and keeps working. Rewarded ads (coins, continue, chest reroll) are optional.
  > The Seasonal chest unlocks limited-time jellies by date from the device clock (Fall Oct 1–Nov 30, Winter Dec 1–Jan 7, Spring Mar 10–Apr 30, Summer Jun 20–Aug 31). Outside those dates it is greyed out and shows a preview of the next season's jellies.
