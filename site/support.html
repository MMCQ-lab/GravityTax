Jelly Swing Arcade – Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/MMCQ-lab/GravityTax/issues and include your device model and iOS version.

FAQ

How do I play? Tap to jump. Hold to grab the nearest purple orb and swing, then let go to fling yourself.

Where are my coins and jellies saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over? Settings → Clear All Data. It asks twice and lists exactly what will be deleted.

How do I turn off the sound? Open Settings on the main menu, or pause during a run.

How do I get new jellies and effects? Earn coins by playing, then open chests in the Jelly Vault. There are no in-app purchases.

Why is the Seasonal chest greyed out? It opens during Fall (Oct 1–Nov 30), Winter (Dec 1–Jan 7), Spring (Mar 10–Apr 30) and Summer (Jun 20–Aug 31). In between it shows a preview of the next season's jellies.

Do ads collect data? Ads come from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show them. They are family-friendly and non-personalized. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I see the tutorial again? Tap the ? button on the home screen.
