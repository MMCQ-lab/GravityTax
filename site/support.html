Jelly Swing Arcade – Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/MMCQ-lab/GravityTax/issues and include your device model and iOS version.

FAQ

How do I play? Tap to jump. Hold to grab the nearest purple orb and swing, then let go to fling yourself.

Where are my coins and jellies saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off the sound? Open Settings on the main menu, or pause during a run.
