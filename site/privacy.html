Jelly Swing Arcade – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Jelly Swing Arcade ("the game") is made by MMCQ-Lab. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in. Your scores, coins, unlocked jellies and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect limited information such as an approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app identifiers, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data. We request that all ads are family-friendly (rated G) and that ad requests are treated as child-directed under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as coming from users under the age of consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. This limits Google to non-personalized advertising. The game does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited as described above. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Your choices

Where required by law, a consent message from Google appears before ads are shown. You can delete all game data at any time by deleting the app.
