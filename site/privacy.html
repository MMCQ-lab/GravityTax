Jelly Swing Arcade – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Jelly Swing Arcade ("the game") is made by MMCQ-Lab. This policy explains what information the game uses and who may collect it.

Important: the game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect data from your device. MMCQ-Lab does not collect any data itself. Details are in the Advertising section below.

Information we (MMCQ-Lab) collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to talk to other players. We do not run any servers for the game, and we do not collect, store, share or sell personal information.

Your progress is saved only on your device: high scores, coins, unlocked jellies, effects, power-ups, settings, and the optional player name you type in Settings. The player name is never sent anywhere; it only appears on your own screen.

Advertising: Google AdMob may collect data

The game shows ads (banner, rewarded and full-screen ads) served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and fix problems, Google may collect and use information from your device, which can include:

  • an approximate (coarse) location worked out from your IP address;
  • device and app identifiers (for example, an identifier for this app on your device);
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • product interaction data, such as app launches and taps;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash reports and performance data.

Google handles this information under its own policies. MMCQ-Lab does not receive it in a way that identifies you. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google advertising.

To keep ads suitable for everyone, the game asks Google to:

  • show only family-friendly ads (content rated G);
  • treat every ad request as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA);
  • treat every ad request as coming from someone under the age of consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

This tells Google not to show personalized (interest-based) ads. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it does not use Apple's advertising identifier for tracking.

Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose whether to watch one for coins, a continue, or a chest reroll.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are limited as described above. Parents with questions can contact us using the details below.

Your choices

  • Clear your game data: Settings → Clear All Data deletes your high scores, jellies, coins, effects, power-ups and name from your device. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Ads: on iPhone and iPad you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in the device's Privacy settings. Where the law requires it, a consent message from Google appears before ads are shown.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
